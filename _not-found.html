Method and sources

Every figure on this page is a server-side aggregation of the official register, embedded at build time.

  • Source: RDW Open Data, datasets m9d7-ebf2, 8ys7-d773, and the Ferrari brand extract pmhw-w82q, license CC0 1.0.
  • Snapshot date: 2026-11-04. The register reflects the current state only, so time series on this page are built from what is on the road today.
  • Aggregations are computed with server-side queries against the RDW API; no raw vehicle records are stored or published. No personal owner data exists in this source.
  • The heavy turn: SoQL averages of massa_ledig_voertuig and cilinderinhoud per first-admission-year cohort of passenger cars from 1975 on. The mass averages cover all passenger cars including electric vehicles, the engine-size averages cover only vehicles with a recorded cilinderinhoud, and cohorts under 1,000 vehicles are excluded.
  • The fleet by fuel: the raw fuel register holds one registration per fuel, 17,045,233 records covering the 15,175,498 vehicles with fuel data. The chart classifies every vehicle once, in this order: battery electric (electricity with no recorded hybrid class), full hybrid and plug-in hybrid (electricity plus a combustion fuel, by the recorded hybrid class, the same classes as the drivetrain treemap), LPG (an LPG record without electricity: petrol cars converted to also run LPG, their distinguishing fuel), petrol (benzine without electricity and without LPG), diesel (diesel without electricity; diesel-plus-LNG duals stay here), and everything else — fuel cell hybrids, hydrogen combustion, CNG, alcohol, LNG — as other. The register is a live administrative dataset, so a small residue of records it does not record cleanly (broken fuel sequences, unclassified hybrids) is grouped under other as well.
  • The fuel cell coverage is a SoQL count of distinct kenteken per recorded fuel over the fuel cell hybrid classes NOVC-FCHV and OVC-FCHV. Hydrogen fuel cell vehicles carry a Waterstof row without a matching Elektriciteit row, which is why the electric split counts only the electricity-listing portion.
  • The closing Ferrari card is derived from a full fetch of the Ferrari brand extract, a filtered view of the same register holding every vehicle with merk FERRARI. Every figure is counted from the raw record values at snapshot time: color is the recorded eerste_kleur, and the model figures match the register's handelsbenaming exactly, which is what makes the 458 and the California the tied most common models. No raw records are stored.
  • The AI insight blocks beneath the charts are generated at data-refresh time by Mistral (mistral-small-latest) from the same verified aggregates that power the charts. Every figure is computed from the official register and only the explanation text is AI-generated; the feature is experimental and a narration regenerates only when the data changes.
  • Entry year is the first registration in the Netherlands, so imported used vehicles count in the year they arrived, not their build year. Because the register holds the current state only, older bars shrink through attrition, not through declining registrations.
  • Figures include all vehicle types unless stated otherwise; trailers and similar vehicles without fuel data are excluded from fuel-based shares: trailers, centre-axle trailers, semi-trailers, and agricultural trailers and trailed equipment have no fuel data, which is why fuel-based figures cover fewer vehicles than the register total. The fuel-data total covers all motor vehicles, mopeds and motorcycles included, so the drivetrain figures are motor vehicle figures, not car figures.
